Lecture: Course Overview & Learning Path
----------------------------------------

Dear friends, hello and welcome to our Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate Preparation course. We have been working in the field of data engineering and big data technologies for a long time, and so far we have trained thousands of students in this area. We are here to both prepare you for this important certification and help you gain strong skills that you can apply in real-world projects. Yes, the primary goal of this course is to get you 100% ready for the Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate exam. But passing the exam is not enough. We also want you to become a data engineer who can use Databricks effectively in professional environments and feel confident in real projects. Throughout the course, you will follow a step-by-step learning journey. First, you will learn how to set up the Databricks environment, navigate the interface, and use the necessary tools. Then we will cover many hands-on topics such as lakehouse architecture, Delta Lake, autoloader, structured streaming, Spark SQL, PySpark, Delta Live Tables, Lakeflow, Jobs, Unity Catalog, Databricks SQL, and dashboard creation. I strongly believe in the learning-by-doing approach. That's why this course is not limited to theory. In each section, we will first explain the topic in a clear and understandable way, then perform hands-on exercises directly in Databricks. At the end of each section, you will test your knowledge with short quizzes. You can start this course from scratch. You don't need any prior knowledge of Databricks. However, if you already know some SQL and Python, you will progress much faster. If you don't, don't worry. I'll explain everything step-by-step. My only expectation from you is to follow the lessons, practice along with the exercises, and not skip the quizzes. By the time you complete this course, you will have all the knowledge and practice required to pass the exam, be able to use Databricks effectively in real projects, and add a valuable certification to your portfolio to boost your career. I truly enjoyed preparing this course, and I believe you will feel the same excitement throughout your learning journey. If you're ready, let's step into the world of Databricks together. See you in the next lesson.
