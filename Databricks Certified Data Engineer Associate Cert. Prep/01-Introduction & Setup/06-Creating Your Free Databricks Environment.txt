Lecture: Creating Your Free Databricks Environment
----------------------------------------

Dear friends, hello everyone. In this lesson, we'll go step-by-step through how to create your Databricks Free Edition account. We'll complete this process entirely free of charge using just an email address. By the end, we'll have our own Databricks environment ready. First, let's take a look at what Databricks Free Edition is. This version is a free environment designed specifically for learning and practice. Now, some of you might be wondering, we've also heard about something called Community Edition. What is that? Community Edition was the very first free learning environment Databricks offered for a long time, but its main purpose was mostly to introduce Apache Spark and teach basic PySpark applications. In other words, it did not provide the full Databricks experience. Its features were quite limited. You could only work with simple notebooks and small datasets. Modern tools like Delta Lake, Autoloader, or Unity Catalog were not available. If you search Databricks Community Edition on Google, you'll see the page. But with a warning, Community Edition will shut down soon and it redirects you to Free Edition. That's why we'll be using Free Edition from now on. So, what is Free Edition? Free Edition replaced Community Edition as a more updated and powerful option. It provides an interface and user experience much closer to the paid version of Databricks. Here, you don't just run Spark, you also get hands-on access to production-grade features like Delta Lake, Autoloader, SQL Warehouse, Unity Catalog, and more. All right, let's go ahead and create our account. First, open your browser and search for Databricks Free Edition. You'll see several options. Click on the first one. On the page that opens, you'll only be asked for an email address. This can be Gmail, Outlook, or any personal email. You don't need a work email or credit card information. Once you sign up, Databricks will automatically create a workspace, your working environment, for you. I'll continue with Google. After selecting my account, I see two options. The For Work option on the left offers a 14-day free trial with $400 credit, but it becomes paid afterward. The For Personal Use option on the right is the Free Edition, completely free, no time limit, and perfect for learning. That's the one we need, so click on Get Free Edition. Now, it will ask for a name and a location. For example, you can enter any name you like, and for location, simply choose your country. Click Continue, and you'll see your account being set up. Once it's ready, you'll be signed in. Sometimes, the system may ask you to solve a puzzle to confirm that you're not a bot. If that happens, just complete it to proceed. In my case, it didn't appear. And there we go. As you can see, we're now inside the platform. With your Free Edition account, you can open notebooks, run SQL queries, build simple data pipelines, and create visualization. Everything here is free of charge. However, since resources are limited, it's not suitable for very large data sets or long-running processes. In conclusion, Databricks Free Edition is the perfect starting point for your learning journey. In the next lesson, we'll explore the inside of this platform together. See you in the next one.
